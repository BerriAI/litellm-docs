Artwork is original code: a shaded parametric surface with ordered Bayer
dithering in artwork.js. It is decorative, not a model architecture diagram.
The page follows Docusaurus's theme setting; no separate preference is stored.
Geist and the LiteLLM mark use the repository's existing official assets.

To reproduce the cover, serve the repository root with a static file server,
open blog/abliterated-models/cover.html at 1200 x 630, wait for fonts, and export
a PNG screenshot. Add ?theme=light for the light edition. Optimize both PNGs
before replacing static/img/blog/abliterated-models/cover.png and cover-light.png.
The image and optional image_light frontmatter supply the theme-aware listing.
The dark edition remains the social preview, whose viewers do not supply a theme.
