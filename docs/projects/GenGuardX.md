# GenGuardX

[GenGuardX](https://genguardx.ai) by Corridor Platforms is an AI governance platform for regulated industries such as banks, credit unions and healthcare. Teams use it to test, approve and monitor GenAI agents, with the evidence their risk and compliance reviews need.

LiteLLM Proxy is a built-in LLM gateway in GenGuardX: administrators point GenGuardX at their LiteLLM Proxy, and the agents and evaluation judges in GenGuardX call every model through it, with the proxy's keys, budgets and routing.
